Government Medical College, Mahabubabad

A government MBBS institution serving Mahabubabad district and the surrounding rural and tribal regions of Telangana.

About the College

Government Medical College (GMC), Mahabubabad is a state-run medical institution in Mahabubabad district, Telangana. It was among eight new government medical colleges whose first MBBS academic year was inaugurated in 2022, alongside Sangareddy, Mancherial, Jagtial, Wanaparthy, Kothagudem, Nagarkurnool and Ramagundam. The initiative is part of the state's plan to have a medical college in every district.

The college is affiliated with Kaloji Narayana Rao University of Health Sciences (KNRUHS), Warangal, and is approved by the National Medical Commission (NMC) for its MBBS intake. It trains students through its teaching hospital, giving warly clinical exposure to a wide mix of rural and tribal patients.

Admissions

  • Entrance: Admission is strictly through NEET-UG scores.
  • State quota (85%): Allotted via Telangana MBBS counselling run by KNRUHS, for local candidates.
  • All India Quota (15%): Allotted through MCC counselling.
  • Choice filling:Select "Government Medical College, Mahabubabad" during web options.
  • Fees: As a government college, fees are among the most affordable for MBBS in the state. Check KNRUHS/MCC notices for the current fee schedule and bond terms.

Teaching Hospital

The college is attachedto the Government General Hospital, Mahabubabad which provides students with hands-on clinical training. Listings describe it as a 300+ bed hospital serving patients from rural and tribal areas across the district and beyond.

  • Early and broad clinical exposure for MBBS students
  • Compulsary one-year rotating internship at the teaching hospital
  • Public healthcare services for the district population
